# Berean design guide

**Status:** v3 approved by Owen (Oct 28, 2026), including Omnisearch as one search with two ways in. This refresh applies Owen's two Notes corrections: highlights are colors only (no category names), and notes are Markdown with folders and tags. This is the reference that every platform builds against: SwiftUI on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS (Kiwi, Moss), and the Expo app on iOS and Android (Otter).

- [berean-design-guide.pdf](berean-design-guide/berean-design-guide.pdf): an instant PDF export, three very tall pages.
  - Rebuilt from the HTML on Oct 31, 2026 (headless Chrome, same page size), so it now matches the HTML, including the folder-delete copy. Fallback fonts differ slightly from Claude Design's export; the HTML stays authoritative.
- [berean-design-guide.html](berean-design-guide/berean-design-guide.html): a standalone HTML export. Open it in Safari on a Mac to see New York render; other browsers fall back to Georgia.
- Source: a Claude Design project in Owen's account (private). Claude Design moves to Artifacts in the Claude app, and this version runs until Dec 14, 2026, so these exports are the durable copy.

Built with Kerning & Co. (Iris, Nova, Hinge, Pebble).

## Sections
1. **Foundations:** type, color tokens, spacing, and the material rule.
   - Type: system UI fonts. The reading serif is New York on Apple and bundled Literata on Android, with Noto Serif Hebrew one step larger for Hebrew.
   - Color: superseded by section 8, Themes. The single palette (light, dark, sepia and Increase Contrast sets, highlights at equal OKLCH lightness, text on highlights at least 4.5:1, "Highlighted, yellow") no longer applies. The outline token now comes per theme from the theme tokens (#8B8275 in Paper light is unchanged). Differentiate Without Color keeps its margin shapes (circle, square, triangle, diamond, bar, ring).
   - Spacing: a 4 pt grid.
   - Liquid Glass goes only on controls. Content always sits on solid surfaces.
2. **Core components.**
3. **Reader with the inline study pane:** iPhone and Android phone inline; Mac and iPad docked side split with snaps at 1/3, 1/2, and 2/3, pin, and dock (⌘\); Android tablet in Material's supporting-pane layout at 840 dp and wider, with secondary tabs that become a menu at large font scales; Android foldable in book posture. See [inline-study-pane.md](inline-study-pane.md).
4. **Studyspace:** the full canvas on iPad and Mac, fit-all with an Add sheet on iPhone, and a List view on every platform.
   - Feel specs (pan and zoom physics, snapping, selection, Omnisearch landing, note links) and reader cards (live publication windows on the canvas) are in [studyspace-findings.md](studyspace-findings.md).
   - Change since v3 (Wren, Oct 31, 2026): iPhone opens a workspace at fit-all only the first time, then reopens the last view. Zoom runs 10–800% on iPad and Mac and 10–400% on phones; only fit-all may go below 10%. The HTML and PDF exports still show v3.
5. **Omnisearch and note hyperlinks:** one search with two ways in. The main way in is the platform's native search: a search tab at the trailing end of the glass tab bar on iPhone, a toolbar search field on iPad and Mac, and a Material 3 search bar on Android (docked above the navigation bar on phones, in the top app bar on tablets). ⌘K or Ctrl+K opens the same search as a palette, carrying over the query. Previews open on long press on iPhone, iPad and Android, and as a popover on Mac, with a "Download this publication" variant that announces progress and offers Retry.
6. **Adaptation and accessibility:** a matrix across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android phone, Android tablet, and foldable, plus Dynamic Type, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast, VoiceOver and TalkBack, keyboard, and Reduce Motion. The matrix includes a Notes row.
7. **Notes:**
   - 7.1, the list and editor: notes are Markdown, organized in nested folders, with tags from inline #tags or the note's info. On Mac and iPad, there's a sidebar folder tree with All Notes, Recents and Tags, and tag search tokens; drag a note onto a folder to move it. iPhone and Android phone drill in from a Folders list, with filter chips (M3 filter chips on Android). Android tablet has a navigation rail plus a drawer. The column widths are three at 1000 pt and wider and two at 640 to 999 pt. The editor has a Read view (fully rendered) and a Write view (styled live, with syntax dimmed but visible), a formatting bar, and ⌘1 and ⌘2 (Ctrl+1 and Ctrl+2 on Android) to switch views. Notes save as you type, with no Save or Cancel, and a new note left empty is discarded. A note opens in Read from a list and in Write when it's new or its body is empty. The title is a plain-text field above the body. A note started from a verse has Done on iOS and a close X on Material, with Write | Read in the title slot. The formatting bar (heading, bold, italic, list, checklist, quote, link, tag) hides with the keyboard on phones and docks with a hardware keyboard. Passage and publication links are typed or picked through Omnisearch and stored as standard Markdown links. A Markdown source sample sits next to its rendered view. Folder actions with an undo: deleting a folder unfiles its notes (they stay in All Notes, just no longer in that folder) and shows an Undo snackbar or toast. Notes don't go to Recently Deleted in this pass; Recently Deleted is deferred to its own Holding row (#31). Download-row states.
   - 7.2, notes in the reader: margin markers where there's room. Below about 400 pt, a count badge sits on the verse number, and markers on one line merge. Tapping the verse number opens the last-used tab. Tapping a margin marker opens My notes. VoiceOver and TalkBack read "Verse 4, 2 notes", with Study and Show notes actions. It uses custom shared marker glyphs. Underlines are only for links; highlights use a fill and a spoken color name. Exception: Pencil underline annotations on reader cards (Studyspace) draw as a thick coloured stroke in the highlight colour, at least 3 pt and set below the baseline. That's clearly heavier than the 1 pt link underline in body ink, and it's never tappable.
   - 7.3, Studyspace notes: opening a note in Studyspace from the reader, Show in reader, and an arrival highlight.
   - The 640 and 1000 pt column widths are placeholders.
8. **Themes** (decided Oct 31, 2026): the spec is [themes/themes-spec.md](themes/themes-spec.md), and the source of truth for every color is [themes/tokens.json](themes/tokens.json).
   - Four themes: Paper (the default, today's shipped look), Sepia, Stone and Sage. Paper keeps every shipped value that passes the contrast floors; the outlines and highlights change as accessibility fixes. The old sepia reading surface is now the Sepia theme.
   - Each theme has light, dark, and Increase Contrast versions of both. The appearance is Automatic (follows the system, the default), Light or Dark. Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency follow the system settings. The choice is stored per device, and nothing goes in core.
   - Six preset accents per theme, with slot 1 as the default and no free picker. Mac adds an opt-in System accent. Android 12 and later adds opt-in Material You "System colors", for chrome only; the page, scripture text and highlights always use the theme.
   - The accent appears only on the single primary action and on selected states. Glass stays clear, with no accent tint, and the focus ring follows the accent.
   - Highlights are colors only, in six fixed hues, keyed by the core's color names and shown in this order: yellow, green, blue, pink, purple, orange. Each theme tunes only lightness and chroma. Yellow, green, pink and purple are light, and blue and orange are deep, so pairs that color-blind readers confuse also differ in lightness. Text on every highlight is at least 7:1, and screen readers say "Yellow highlight".
   - The picker: the reader itself is the live preview. It's an Aa sheet on iPhone, an Aa popover on iPad and Mac, and a bottom or side sheet on Android, with the full set in Settings.
   - Links are `textPrimary` with an underline, colored by the decorative `linkUnderline` token.
   - Every combination is checked by [themes/contrast.py](themes/contrast.py): 2,448 checks, 0 failures. The frames are in [themes/frames/](themes/frames/) (F01–F10).
   - **The guide's HTML and PDF are not rebuilt for this section.** They still show the earlier single palette, so this Markdown and the themes spec take precedence until they're re-exported.
