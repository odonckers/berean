## Who it's for
People who think spatially on iPad and Mac and collect media, PDFs, and notes onto nested boards ([Muse for Mac](https://allume.com/memos/2022-03-muse-for-mac/)).

## Pricing (https://allume.com/pricing)
- Free: 2 workspaces, 100 cards per workspace, 1 collaborator.
- Pro: $9.99/month or $99.99/year.
- Setapp: $14.99/month.

## Core flow and focus areas
1. **Placement.** Drag text, images, PDFs, and files in. Boards nest. An inbox sits at the left edge. Excerpt works on an area of an image, PDF, or video: "Context menu → Excerpt" ([Muse for Mac](https://allume.com/memos/2022-03-muse-for-mac/)).
2. **Citations back to source (Source Peek).** "Click the circle in the lower-left corner of the excerpt card to see the context in the source. Pinch in or double click to zoom to the source" ([Muse for Mac](https://allume.com/memos/2022-03-muse-for-mac/)). This is the best-in-class clip-to-source pattern we found.
3. **Snapping and edges.** "Create connections between cards" ([pricing](https://allume.com/pricing)). We found no docs on snapping.
