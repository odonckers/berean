## Who it's for
Freeform spatial thinking: brainstorming, planning, and moodboards with cards, connections, and boxes.

## Pricing (https://kinopio.club/help/how-much-does-kinopio-cost)
- "All features of Kinopio are free to use and collaborate with, up to a limit of 100 cards and an upload file size limit of 5mb."
- "Kinopio costs $8/month, $80/year, or $250/life (USD) for unlimited cards and uploads." The iOS price is higher.

## Focus areas
1. **Placement.** Cards are created on the canvas. Bulletin 36 adds "Auto Align Cards Below": "When editing cards, the vertically aligned cards below will automatically adjust positions to stay aligned and prevent overlapping" ([bulletin 36](https://kinopio.club/blog/posts/36/)).
2. **Citations.** No source-link model documented.
3. **Snapping and edges.**
   - "Shift-Drag to Snap to Grid… Helpful when you want to line things up perfectly." "Snap Guide Lines" appear while shift-dragging, "to help maintain horizontal or vertical alignment" ([bulletin 36](https://kinopio.club/blog/posts/36/)).
   - Boxes: "If you drag a box near the outer edge of another box you'll snap them together. Handy for making grids, tables, and calendars" ([boxes](https://kinopio.club/help/boxes)).
   - Connections are drawn by dragging, with optional labels that can be repositioned ([connections](https://kinopio.club/help/editing-connections)).
