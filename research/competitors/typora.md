## Who it's for
Writers who want a seamless WYSIWYG markdown editor for individual files.

## Pricing (https://store.typora.io)
$14.99 one-time for up to 3 devices, with a 15-day trial.

## Linking, backlinks, citations
- Standard markdown links to URLs, local files, and headings, e.g. `[Readme1](Readme1.md#header-1)`. `#` links jump to headings in the same file. Cmd/Ctrl+Click opens a link ([Links](https://support.typora.io/Links/)).
- We found no wikilinks or backlinks in the help pages we read (not found, rather than proven absent).
- No citation feature found.

## Broken link display
Typora reports a broken link with an error when you try to open it. The FAQ lists:
- "Cannot open location /abc.com, do you mean http://abc.com?"
- "Cannot open location file.md, would you want to create file at folder/file.md and continue?"

([Links](https://support.typora.io/Links/))
