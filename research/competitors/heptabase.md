## Who it's for
Researchers and learners who read sources (PDFs, web pages) and build understanding on whiteboards.

## Pricing (https://heptabase.com/pricing)
- Pro: $8.99/month, billed yearly.
- Premium: $17.99/month, billed yearly.
- Yearly billing saves 25%. We didn't capture the monthly-billing price from the official page.

## Core flow and focus areas
1. **Placement.**
   - Cards live in a Card Library, and the same card can sit on many whiteboards. Card Info lists which whiteboards it's on, and clicking one "will automatically open the whiteboard and focus on the location of the card" ([fundamentals](https://wiki.heptabase.com/fundamental-elements)).
   - Right-click empty space to create cards, text, mind maps, sections, and sub-whiteboards ([fundamentals](https://wiki.heptabase.com/fundamental-elements)).
   - Drag a PDF onto a whiteboard. Highlight text or areas to make Highlight Cards, then drag highlights from the right sidebar onto the whiteboard ([PDF annotation](https://wiki.heptabase.com/pdf-annotation)).
2. **Citations back to source.**
   - You can "locate these highlights back to their original content positions" in the PDF ([PDF annotation](https://wiki.heptabase.com/pdf-annotation)).
   - A highlight can be embedded in a note via Copy link → Paste as embed ([PDF annotation](https://wiki.heptabase.com/pdf-annotation)).
3. **Snapping and edges.**
   - Connect tool (`C`) draws arrows between cards and objects ([fundamentals](https://wiki.heptabase.com/fundamental-elements), [shortcuts](https://wiki.heptabase.com/keyboard-shortcuts)).
   - Hold Shift while dragging to extend the snapping range of alignment guides ([shortcuts](https://wiki.heptabase.com/keyboard-shortcuts)). The Sept 2023 newsletter says Shift moves cards along a hidden grid and straightens the line between connected cards ([newsletter](https://wiki.heptabase.com/newsletters/2023-09-28)).
   - "Better alignment guide" is on the roadmap ([nextup](https://wiki.heptabase.com/roadmap/nextup)).

## Broken link display
The CLI docs say a mention of the wrong type can show "Invalid card" ([CLI](https://support.heptabase.com/en/articles/14715462-heptabase-cli)). We found no doc on how the app displays a deleted or missing card.

## Notes ↔ drawings
There is no freehand drawing today. The roadmap lists "Whiteboard: Handwriting", "Whiteboard: Shape elements", "Editor: Handwriting blocks", and "PDF: Handwriting and text" ([nextup](https://wiki.heptabase.com/roadmap/nextup)).
