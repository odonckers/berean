## Who it's for
Bible students, pastors, and teachers who want to diagram a passage visually inside a large Bible-study library.

## Pricing
**Unverified on an official page.**
- https://www.logos.com/pricing returned 404, and the subscriptions configurator didn't render prices for our fetcher.
- Official pages disagree on the required tier. The feature page says Canvas is included "in Bronze and up" ([feature page](https://www.logos.com/features/canvas)). The help article says it needs a "Premium subscription or higher" ([help](https://support.logos.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018175571-Visually-Engage-the-Biblical-Text-Using-the-Canvas-Tool)).

## Onboarding
Not evaluated (no sign-up).

## Core flow and focus areas
1. **Placement.** You add a biblical passage to a Canvas, then drag portions of text, add shapes, freehand drawing, and info cards that link to Factbook and Bible Word Study. A canvas is saved as a Logos document and can be exported as an image ([help](https://support.logos.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018175571-Visually-Engage-the-Biblical-Text-Using-the-Canvas-Tool), [feature page](https://www.logos.com/features/canvas)). Inserted text keeps Logos data tagging, and info cards follow the user's prioritized Bibles ([thread 173729](https://community.logos.com/discussion/173729/canvas)).
2. **Citations and labels.** Canvas does not label the reference automatically. In 2019 a user reported that inserting a passage shows only verse numbers, so the reference has to be typed into a text box. Unlike Notes, it doesn't auto-hyperlink; it was called "a little cumbersome," with "not much development since L8" ([thread 187006](https://community.logos.com/discussion/187006/adding-scripture-references-in-canvas)).
3. **Snapping and edges.**
   - Canvas has snap-to-grid "Guides" that can be turned off: uncheck "Guides" in the document menu. A Sept 2024 user wanted cards to float free ([thread 225386](https://community.logos.com/discussion/225386/canvas-turn-off-snap-to-grid/p1)).
   - When dragging Bible text lines, the line-spacing snap was lost after a misdrop, and "distribute vertically" didn't apply inside a Text Flow group ([thread 177810](https://community.logos.com/discussion/177810/canvas-struggling-with-line-spacing-snap/p1), Jan 2019).
   - Arrows and connectors: shapes are documented. We didn't verify a dedicated connector tool.

## Translation labeling
- **Canvas.** No automatic translation or reference label, per user evidence ([thread 187006](https://community.logos.com/discussion/187006/adding-scripture-references-in-canvas)).
- **Copy Bible Verses.** This is the separate feature for quoting into documents. You pick the Bible version and a style, and the format tokens include `[Version]` (e.g. ESV). An example footer is `([FullPassageRef], [Version])` ([help](https://support.logos.com/hc/en-us/articles/360015518272-Copy-Bible-Verses-from-Logos), [wiki](https://community.logos.com/kb/articles/863-copy-bible-verses)). The user picks the template, so the label is opt-in.

## Broken citation display
Not found in public docs.

## Notes ↔ drawings
Freehand drawing sits on the same canvas as the passage text, so the drawing *is* the annotation layer ([help](https://support.logos.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018175571-Visually-Engage-the-Biblical-Text-Using-the-Canvas-Tool)). We found no evidence of linking a drawing to a separate note.

## Parallel texts
We found no public evidence of users lining up parallel translations on Canvas. The line-spacing-snap thread (177810) is the closest. It shows that snapping text lines precisely is fiddly.

## Gaps
- Passage clips don't label their reference or version.
- Canvas looks under-developed according to users (thread 187006).
- iPad only on tablets (not Android tablets) ([help](https://support.logos.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018175571-Visually-Engage-the-Biblical-Text-Using-the-Canvas-Tool)).
- A Canvas crash was fixed in v53 (Aug 2026) ([release notes](https://community.logos.com/release-notes/release-notes/technical-notes/logos-desktop/v53/v53_0/)).
