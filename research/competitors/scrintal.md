## Who it's for
Visual note-takers who combine docs and boards ("Visually link ideas to structure your thoughts", [pricing page](https://scrintal.com/pricing)).

## Pricing
**Unverified.** The pricing page shows a "Scrintal Pro $ /month billed annually" plan, but the number is JS-rendered and wasn't captured. Listed features: unlimited docs and boards, unlimited file uploads, real-time collaboration ([pricing](https://scrintal.com/pricing)).

## Focus areas: the key distinction is links vs connectors
- "Links are bi-directional connections between items in your library… global, meaning they connect docs across your entire library." You create one by typing `+` and a doc name. Backlinks show at the bottom of the doc.
- "Connectors are visual lines that link items on a board… local, meaning they only exist on the specific board where they're created." They "do not impact the contents of the docs" and "can also be drawn as standalone arrows."
- (All from [links & connectors](https://scrintal.gitbook.io/scrintal-support-docs/working-with-links-and-connectors).)

This is directly relevant to Berean's edges decision: Scrintal separates the *semantic* link (stored in content) from the *visual* connector (stored per board).
