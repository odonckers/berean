## Who it's for
Obsidian users who type JW publication citations (e.g. `w15 2/15`, `it-1 450`) and want them linked to wol.jw.org. The listing shows 255 downloads as of 2026-10-26 ([plugin page](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/inrefens)).

## Pricing
Free, MIT-licensed ([plugin page](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/inrefens)).

## Core flow
- "Publication citations are automatically detected in both Reading View and Live Preview (Edit mode), and updated as you type." There's no command to run.
- "Every detected citation becomes a clickable link. Clicking opens the result of the search for the citation on wol.jw.org."
- It accepts many citation forms: `w15 2/15`, `w 15 2/15`, `w2015 2/15`, page ranges, `it-1 Abraham`.
- 25+ languages, and a configurable link color. (All from the [plugin page](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/inrefens).)

## Focus areas
1. **Placement.** Not applicable. It decorates text in place.
2. **Citations.** The link goes to a WOL *search* for the citation, not a direct paragraph deep link ([plugin page](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/inrefens)).
3. **Snapping and edges.** Not applicable.

## Broken or undetected citation display
Known limitations ([plugin page](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/inrefens)):
- "A bare `it` (without `-1` or `-2`) is not linked." An undetected citation simply stays plain text, with no warning.
- In Edit mode, a citation containing markup "renders as adjacent links rather than one link."
