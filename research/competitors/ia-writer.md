## Linking, backlinks, citations ([Wikilinks](https://ia.net/writer/support/library/wikilinks))
- "In iA Writer for Mac, iPhone and iPad, Wikilinks make it easy to connect…" Windows isn't named.
- Type `[[` for autocomplete. Titles use `[[destination | title]]`. Links to other locations use `[[Location: link]]`.
- "Wikilinks are exported and published as regular text… This allows you to enrich your notes with connections hidden from everyone else."
- Resolution goes to the nearest matching file: same folder, then subfolder, then parent folder.
- Navigation history lets you go back.
- **Backlinks: unverified.** A search summary says there are none; we didn't open the source.
- No citation feature found. Content blocks embed images, CSV tables, and text files ([content blocks](https://ia.net/writer/support/library/content-blocks)).
