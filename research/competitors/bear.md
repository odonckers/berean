## Linking, backlinks, citations
- Type `[[` for note-title autocomplete. Link to a heading by adding `/` after the note title ([linking FAQ](https://bear.app/faq/how-to-link-notes-together/)).
- Backlinks appear in the Info panel ([info panel FAQ](https://bear.app/faq/how-to-use-the-info-panel-table-of-contents-and-backlinks-in-bear/)).
- A community thread reports that backlinks don't track links made with a note's unique-ID URL ([community 14226](https://community.bear.app/t/backlinks-do-not-work-for-links-created-using-bear-note-unique-id/14226)). We found this through search and didn't read it in full.
- No citation feature found.
